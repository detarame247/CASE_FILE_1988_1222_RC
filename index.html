II. INCIDENT OVERVIEW & URA INVESTIGATION FINDINGS

On the morning of December 22, 1988, the Subject, an enrolled Tracen Academy
trainee known by the stage name Rare Chance, took her own life. Later that
day her body was discovered by her older brother, who had been hired as a
Tracen Academy trainer on that same day.

The URA Investigation Department found no indication of third-party
involvement, forced entry, or any other criminal element. The details of the
manner of death are held in the sealed medical annex and are intentionally
not reproduced in this file.

The investigation found that the Subject had been under severe and prolonged
pressure arising from her unique origin, her physical limitations, and the
expectations that others placed on her. The Office of the Chairperson
subsequently ordered the incident concealed to prevent panic among other
Umamusume facing similar uncertainty about their debut. The Subject's
brother, requested anonymity, and this request has been granted.

III. BACKGROUND & PERSONALITY ASSESSMENT

PERSONALITY:

Subject was not a social person. Staff and classmates consistently describe
her as a very quiet girl who avoided speaking whenever she could. She lived
with persistent social anxiety and low self-esteem, and found interaction
with others a constant struggle. Even so, she kept trying: she made visible
attempts to engage with classmates and to make friends, despite the cost
these attempts evidently carried for her.

ORIGIN & THE "MIRACLE CHILD" EXPECTATION:

Subject is recorded as a rare case of an Umamusume born to ordinary human
parents. This brought her considerable attention from classmates, teachers
and staff alike. The attention carried with it very high expectations: she
was widely regarded as a "miracle child." For a girl who had never felt her
own worth, those expectations came to be the whole of it.

PHYSICAL LIMITATIONS & DEBUT PROSPECTS:

Because of her purely human origins, Subject's body did not develop the
physical abilities required of a racing Umamusume. Her debut as a racer was
therefore considered almost impossible. Subject was aware of this, and it
frightened her: a failed debut would, in her eyes, destroy the expectations
everyone around her held.

THE 1988 GENERATION:

Oguri Cap transferred to the Central in mid-January 1988, shortly after
Subject's enrollment. Her arrival raised the performance standard for the
entire generation, and by extension raised it still further for Rare Chance,
who was already carrying the heaviest expectations of any trainee her year.

VI. ADMINISTRATIVE DIRECTIVES & ORDERS

  1. 1.
    PUBLIC DISCLOSURE:
    SUSPENDED. The incident is concealed from students,
    staff, press and public. No notice, memorial announcement or registry
    entry is to reference the cause of death. Inquiries are to be referred to
    the Office of the Chairperson.
  2. 2.
    NEXT OF KIN & ANONYMITY:
    The Subject's older brother is to remain
    anonymous in all records. His name is to be withheld from every document
    outside the sealed annex. The Academy is to offer him confidential
    bereavement support and a leave of absence from his newly assumed trainer
    duties for as long as he needs it.
  3. 3.
    RECORDS PROTOCOL:
    All originals are sealed under URA Level 5
    classification. Copies are prohibited. Access requires the written
    approval of the Chairperson.
  4. 4.
    STUDENT WELFARE REVIEW:
    The Academy is to quietly establish confidential
    counseling and check-in procedures for undebuted trainees with physical-
    development concerns, and for trainees with documented social anxiety,
    without reference to this case. Staff are to receive guidance on
    recognizing withdrawal and sustained silence as possible signs of
    distress.
  5. 5.
    EXPECTATION REVIEW:
    The Academy is to review how terms such as "miracle
    child" and public attention on atypical cases are handled, so that no
    trainee is made to carry an expectation of exceptional success in place
    of ordinary support.

CONFIDENTIAL ATTACHMENT: CONCEALMENT RATIONALE & WELFARE ADDENDUM

MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD (INVESTIGATIVE NOTE #01):

Subject is not the only Umamusume of her generation to face an uncertain path to
debut. Several current trainees share her struggle with physical development and
carry similar fears about failing expectations. The Chairperson's Office judged that
a public account of this case could spread
panic and distress among those students
,
and ordered its concealment on those grounds.

The Investigation Department records for the file that
concealment must not mean
inaction
: the students most at risk are the ones the file was sealed to protect, and
they need quiet, practical support, starting with the recommendations in Section VI.
